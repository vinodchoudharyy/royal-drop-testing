The Story of Royal Drop Ventures Founder – Vinod Choudhary

Every entrepreneurial journey starts with a dream. For Vinod Choudhary, the journey began as an engineer with a passion for solving problems and creating meaningful solutions.

After completing his engineering education, Vinod explored technology and business, gaining valuable experience in innovation, planning, and customer-focused thinking. During this time, he realized that even everyday products could become powerful branding tools when combined with quality and creativity.

This vision inspired him to establish Royal Drop Ventures, a company specializing in customized and private-label packaged drinking water. His goal was to provide businesses with premium-quality drinking water while helping them strengthen their brand identity through customized bottle labels.

Under his leadership, Royal Drop Ventures introduced customized packaged drinking water in 250 ml, 500 ml, and 1-liter bottles for hotels, cafes, restaurants, corporate offices, weddings, events, and businesses. The company combines quality, hygiene, and professional branding to deliver products that leave a lasting impression.

Vinod believes that entrepreneurship is about creating value, building trust, and continuously improving. His transition from engineer to entrepreneur reflects his willingness to embrace new challenges and turn innovative ideas into reality.

Today, Royal Drop Ventures continues to grow with a commitment to quality, customization, and customer satisfaction, working toward becoming one of the most trusted names in customized packaged drinking water.

“Engineering taught me how to solve problems. Entrepreneurship taught me how to create opportunities. Royal Drop Ventures is where both passions come together.” – Vinod Choudhary
